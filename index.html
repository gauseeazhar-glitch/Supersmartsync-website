A LITTLE MORE CLARITY

Good questions.
Clear answers.

Choosing a growth partner is a big decision. Here are a few things worth knowing.

Ask us anything
Who do you work with?
We focus on SaaS and B2B businesses selling to organizations in the United States and Canada. Whether you are building your first outbound motion or adding support to an established sales team, we tailor the scope to your market and goals.
How do we get started?
Start with a conversation about your business, buyers, and priorities. We then recommend a practical scope, agree on qualification criteria and ways of working, and map the next steps before any outreach begins.
Can you work with our existing tools?
Where practical, we align with your existing CRM, scheduling, and communication tools. Access, reporting, data handling, and handoff responsibilities are agreed together so our work fits into your sales process.
What makes an opportunity qualified?
Qualification is defined with you—not by a generic checklist. It can include account fit, a relevant business need, the contact’s role, genuine interest, and readiness for a next conversation. We share the context your sales team needs for a useful follow-up.

Privacy Policy

How SuperSmartSync collects, uses, and safeguards information when you visit our website or contact our team.

Effective date: May 2025

On this page

1. Overview

SuperSmartSync (“we,” “our,” or “us”) provides B2B sales development, inside sales, lead generation, appointment setting, and business support services from Ahmedabad, Gujarat, India. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle information collected through this website and through direct inquiries made to our team.

2. Information we collect

We collect information that you voluntarily share with us and limited technical information needed to operate the website:

  • Inquiry details: Your name, work email address, company name, phone or WhatsApp number, target market, service interest, and any message you submit through our contact form, email, or phone.
  • Business communications: Information shared when scheduling or conducting discovery calls, scoping conversations, or ongoing client communications.
  • Basic usage data: Standard technical logs (such as browser type, device type, referring pages, and approximate region) used to maintain website security and performance.

3. How we use information

We use the information we collect for legitimate B2B purposes, including to:

  • Respond to your inquiries, questions, and requests for proposals or consultations;
  • Understand your business needs and recommend relevant services;
  • Deliver, manage, and improve our sales development and business support services;
  • Maintain the security, availability, and usability of our website; and
  • Comply with applicable legal or regulatory obligations.

4. Sharing & service providers

We do not sell your personal information. We may share inquiry or business data only with trusted service providers that help us operate our website, email, CRM, scheduling, or communication workflows, or where required by law. Where we process prospect or customer information on behalf of a client, that work is governed by the scope and instructions agreed with that client.

5. Data security & retention

We use reasonable administrative, technical, and organizational safeguards to protect information against unauthorized access, loss, or misuse. We retain inquiry and business records only for as long as needed to fulfill the purposes described in this policy, maintain our business relationship, or meet legal requirements.

6. Your choices & rights

Depending on your location, you may request access to, correction of, or deletion of personal information you have provided to us, or ask us to stop contacting you. To make a request, please email us at team@supersmartsync.com.
